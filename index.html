01 / THE IDEA

Not another place
to be perfect.

PAUSE is a small digital wellbeing space designed for teenagers who sometimes feel overwhelmed by school, expectations, relationships, or everyday life.

It isn't about having the perfect answer. It's about noticing what is going on, choosing one helpful action, and remembering that asking for support is okay.
